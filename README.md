# Jesus Bible Study

A free Bible study page. Open it here: https://jedougherty.github.io/jesus-bible-study/

On an iPhone or iPad: open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then tap **Add to Home Screen**.

- Each Bible version belongs to its publisher. Free, personal study only. The NIV is not offered: its publisher does not allow free use.
- The Bible words come from https://bolls.life and the commentaries from https://bible.helloao.org.
- Your notes and bookmarks stay on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
- The page is one file (index.html). It loads no other programs, and its safety rules only let it talk to those two Bible websites.
