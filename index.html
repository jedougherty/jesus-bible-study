The Greek Old Testament, word by word

Click a word for its meaning. Click the yellow words to hear them.

The Septuagint: the Old Testament put into Greek before Jesus was born. The New Testament writers often quoted it.

The Greek words are from Brenton's Septuagint. The meaning under each word comes from Thayer's Greek dictionary. Thayer's was made for the New Testament, so some Old Testament words have no meaning shown. The yellow sound-it-out guide is worked out from how the word is spelled in this verse. The Greek was translated from an older Hebrew copy, so it can say a verse a little differently than your English Bible.
